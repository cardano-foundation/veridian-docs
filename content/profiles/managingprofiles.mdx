# Managing Profiles

From the profile switcher, you may access the profile management page where you can view profile details such as the identifier, keys or group members.
There is also an option to share your profile with others.

## Editing your profile

Within this page, you can edit the username or group name of any profile.

## Key rotation

As mentioned [previously](../beforeyoustart#rotating-keys), you may want to rotate the keys which relate to your profile.
This helps keep your profile secure in case your signing keys have been compromised.

This can be done from either the home page or the profile management page.
For now, you cannot rotate keys for a group profile, but it's on the near-term roadmap.

## Deleting your profile

If you no longer require a profile, you can choose to delete it.
Recovery at this point is still technically possible, but it wouldn't be straight-forward and is not supported with our wallet currently.

Please ensure you only delete profiles that you will never need again!
Deleting your profile will delete any data related to that profile.

<Callout type="info">
  In KERI, you can "abandon" an identifier by rotating to empty keys but we have not implemented this yet.
  If implemented, deletion would mean your identifier is not recoverable, but also means that attackers can't impersonate you using your old, deleted identifiers.
</Callout>

If you delete all of your profiles, you will once again be prompted to create a profile.
