# Your profile

Within each profile, the respective credentials, connections and notifications will be shown, along with a tab for the home page.

## Home page

The home page on the left provides the user with quick access to various features.
The feature set shown on the home page will differ between Individual and Group profiles, as [Cardano connect](../features/cardanoconnect) and key rotation are currently both only available for Individual profiles.

### Recovery phrase

The home page will also display a blue banner to verify your recovery phrase.
This is an 18-word recovery phrase which should be written down and stored in a secure location — in case you ever lose your phone, it will be required to recover your digital identity on your new device.

For technical readers, the recovery phrase is a BIP-39 conversion of a salt in the KERI ecosystem, which is different from Cardano crypto wallets.
These salts are not quite the right length for BIP-39, so they have been padded with zeros to convert to 18 words. 
You might notice `abandon` appears often towards the end of generated recovery phrases, but don’t worry — this padding is not used for cryptographic functions.

<Callout type="warning">
  Right now, this recovery phrase is the only way to restore your wallet if your device is lost or reset.
  Going forward, we will be exploring how to use multi-sig identifiers for multi-device and social-recovery.
  And don’t worry, your existing identifiers will be migratable from the recovery phrase.
</Callout>

After you have written down your recovery phrase, you can verify that you have written it down correctly.
Once that is complete, the blue banner will go away.

After onboarding, you must complete the verification of your recovery phrase within 2 weeks.
This time limit drops to 1 day in case you perform a number of critical actions.

## Switching profiles

Users can navigate to the profile switcher by clicking the profile icon on the top-right corner of the screen.
Here they will see a list of all profiles, and be able to add new profiles if they'd like.

### Notifications

In case you have unread notifications on another profile, you will be able to see an indicator the profile switcher.
In addition, if you receive a notification to a profile you are not currently viewing, a push notification will be sent to your device if enabled.
Clicking on the push notification will automatically navigate you to the notifications tab of the respective profile, if it still exists.

<Callout type="info">
  For now, push notifications are only received while the app is opened as an indicator to check your other profiles.
</Callout>
