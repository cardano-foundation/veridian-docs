# Settings

To access the app settings, first navigate to the profile swticher by clicking on the profile icon on the top-right corner.
Click on the settings button located at the bottom of the screen to access the Settings page.

From here, you will find general settings, some useful links, as well as a way to delete your wallet.

## Security
The security section allows you to manage your biometrics, PIN, and password.
The password can be enabled, disabled or updated.

It also allows you to view your recovery phrase in case you have lost physical access to it.

<Callout type="info">
  As before, if your device does not support secure biometrics, the biometric options will not be visible in this menu.
</Callout>

## Notifications

Toggling the slider enables or disables push notifications for the wallet.

## Account deletion
Deleting your account will delete all profiles, connections and credentials and allow you to restart the onboarding flow.
You can still use your recovery phrase to gain access to your cloud agent, but the wallet will not contain any data and appear as if it were freshly onboarded.
