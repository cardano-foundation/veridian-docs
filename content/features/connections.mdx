# Connections

Connections can be used to interact with other wallets or services.
For example, you may connect to an issuer to receive a credential.
You may create new connections within any profile, or alternatively you may share your profile with others by way of a connection QR code.
You can even add the same connection within multiple profiles.

If any connections you add cannot be established right away, it will be marked as pending and will eventually complete in the background.
If not, you can always delete the pending connection.

<Callout type="info">
  The connection QR code is effectively an endpoint on your cloud agent.
  For technical folk, this is known as an [Out of Band Introduction](https://trustoverip.github.io/kswg-keri-specification/#out-of-band-introduction-oobi) (OOBI) and is a way to bootstrap a secure connection with an identifier.
  There is more information [here](../foundations/oobis) for developers.
</Callout>

## Your first connection

To demonstrate how connections work, we will use our Credential Issuance tooling that comes bundled with our infrastructure deployments.
For development, we use this [URL](https://cred-issuance-ui.dev.idw-sandboxes.cf-deployments.org/) — however, it is not stable or intended for production!

<Callout type="warning">
  The issuer tool is intended to aid the development of the wallet, and empower quick integration of credentials into your use cases.
  It's not a full-fledged credential issuance tool that is ready for production deployments.
</Callout>

<Steps>
### Add the issuer connection

In the issuer tool, make sure you are on the Connections page.
The `Add Connection` button may be selected to display the connection QR code of the tool.

From the wallet, you may navigate to the connections tab of the profile which you'd like to connect to the tool.
From here, simply scan the connection QR code, and a new connection should be added called "CF Credential Issuance".

You can also add a connection directly from the home page.

<Callout type="info">
  Once connected, all communications will be end-to-end encrypted with incredibly robust security using a technique called Encrypt Sender and Sign Receiver.
  This provides particular security advantages over other protocols like DIDComm; technical folk can read more [here](https://github.com/SmithSamuelM/Papers/blob/master/whitepapers/SPAC_Message.md#best-combined-authenticity-and-confidentiality).
</Callout>

### Share our profile

The connection QR code of your wallet may be found on the "Provide" tab of the connections scanner you just used.
It is also located within the profile management page under the share option.

Now, within the issuer tool, you can click the next button in the connect flow.
From here you will have the choice of using your webcam to scan your phone, or pasting the connection URL.

You are now ready to start interacting with that connection — in this case, you would be able to [receive credentials](./credentials).

<Callout type="info">
  Technically, this step can be skipped by simply reloading the page in your browser.
  This is because our wallet auto-creates a connection with the tool under the hood.

  We have kept the above instructions in case you encounter a tool which does not allow that.
</Callout>

### View connection

Within the details of a connection you will find a history of interactions.
This can include activities such as being issued a credential, or receiving a request to present one.

You can also add some notes to the connection. These are completely personal and are never shared with the other party.

Finally, if you'd like to remove a connection you can do so from this screen.
Deleting a connection will remove the connection from that profile, and permanently delete the connection history items or notes for that profile.

</Steps>

## Unknown connections

Sometimes you might receive a notification from an unknown connection, or from a previously deleted connection.
You won't be able to action on these notifications until you add, or re-add the connection.
We believe in providing as much feedback as possible to the user in case they forget to complete the connection flow.
For spam prevention, we will likely block these from appearing once we have the interactive connection flow developed!
