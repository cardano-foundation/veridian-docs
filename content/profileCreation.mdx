# Profile Creation

The Veridian wallet allows you to create multiple profiles to manage your digital identities. 
After completing the onboarding process, you can choose to create either an **Individual** or a **Group** profile.

## Individual Group

To set up an individual profile, simply enter a name for the new profile. 
Once you confirm the name, your profile is ready for immediate use.

## Group Profile

Group Profiles are represented by threshold multi-sig identifiers.
The creation of a group profile builds on the idea of connections, as all members will need a way to coordinate the creation and usage of the group profile. Remember — there’s no central platform for coordination!

<Steps>
### Initiator setup

One member acts as the **Initiator** by selecting the "Group Profile" type and assigning it a name. 
The Initiator then chooses their own username within that group. 
Once initialized, a connection QR code will appear, which can be shared with the other prospective members.

### Member setup

To join, other members must scan the Initiator’s QR code via the "Join a Group" option in the Veridian wallet's profile menu. 
After scanning and choosing their own group username, they can proceed to the next step.

### Remaining QR exchanges

Now, each group member will have their own QR code — initiator and non-initiators alike.
To ensure a secure, decentralized setup, each member (excluding the Initiator) must connect with all other members. 
You can track connected members at the bottom of your screen.

Note that you will only see members whose QR codes you have personally scanned. For the group to be valid, every member must scan everyone else's code.

<Callout type="info">
  This first iteration of group creation was intended to be flexible and secure, but we are aware of the complexities in scanning so many QR codes.
  It's quite easy for a group of 2, or maybe 3, but quickly gets harder.
  
  We have a design in place to implement that will drastically reduce the QR codes scanned, and use broadcasting under the hood to propagate the group member list.
  However we do it, we want to keep security as a priority.
</Callout>

### Finalize setup

Once all members are interconnected, the Initiator can finalize the group profile. 
At this point, the Initiator can remove any undesired participants and define the **signing and recovery thresholds**. 
The threshold determines the minimum number of signatures required to authorize transactions, rotate keys, or recover the profile.

### Confirm group profile settings

Finally, all members receive a notification from the Initiator to review and accept the group settings. 
The group profile is officially created once the number of members accepting the request meets the defined threshold.

</Steps>

Once created, every member can access the group profile from their wallet and begin using it.
